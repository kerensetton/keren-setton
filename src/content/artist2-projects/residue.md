Residue is an installation and photographic series made in response to a single question: what does an object remember? Working with materials gathered from flea markets, demolition sites, and abandoned apartments across eastern Germany, the work investigates the idea of use — not function, but the physical evidence that handling leaves behind.

The installation component arranged over 200 found objects — worn tools, children's toys, domestic utensils — on low white shelving, organized by material rather than category. Visitors were invited to handle anything they wished. The photographic series documents these objects in extreme close-up, lit to emphasize surface rather than form: scratches, discoloration, the soft depression left by a hand held too many times.

Together, the works propose that objects are a kind of archive — not of events, but of the bodies that moved through them.
